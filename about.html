Why this exists

Philosophy.

Budgeting is the wrong constraint

Optimizing spend doesn't move the needle when income is the binding constraint. The shift is from "spend less" to "earn more per hour of invested time."

W2 employment doesn't scale

More hours don't produce more income in a salaried role. Non-linear income requires building something that earns while you're not working.

Failure is the data

The lessons from failures outweigh the lessons from success. Rapid iteration and willingness to kill ideas fast are the core discipline — not optimism.

The framework is the asset

No single idea needs to be a home run. A portfolio of semi-automated initiatives each clearing the 1.5× floor compounds regardless of individual outcomes.
